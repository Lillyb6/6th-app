# Initial roadmap

## Milestone 1: Photo to interpretation

- Verify the existing Flutter platform projects on a device.
- Add camera and photo-library selection.
- Define an upload contract with file-size and image-type limits.
- Add server-side Gemini image interpretation.
- Show loading, retry, and error states.
- Let the user enter a guess before revealing the AI suggestion.

## Milestone 2: Personal collection

- Choose authentication and image storage.
- Save photos, guesses, and AI interpretations.
- Add collection browsing and deletion.

## Milestone 3: Share and debate

- Share a cloud through an invite link.
- Let friends submit guesses and vote.
- Add reporting and moderation before public sharing.

## Product principles

- AI suggestions are playful interpretations, not definitive answers.
- Preserve room for users to see something different.
- Keep credentials on the server.
- Decide photo retention and privacy behavior before storing user uploads.
